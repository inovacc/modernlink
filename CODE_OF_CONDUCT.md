# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ModernLink is committed to a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless
of experience, identity, background, ability, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, or other
personal characteristic.

Be respectful and constructive. Technical disagreement is expected; personal attacks, harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or imagery, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
documentation, and other project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to
[dyam.marcano@gmail.com](mailto:dyam.marcano@gmail.com) with the subject
`[ModernLink conduct]`. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or take other
appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior. Good-faith reports and participation in
an investigation must not lead to retaliation.
